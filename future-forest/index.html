Why it matters

Giving back for everything nature has given us

The Future Forest brings the whole mission together: wildlife rehabilitation and species preservation, a global collection of remarkable trees, research and education, healthcare for the community, and clean energy and water systems, all coordinated from a conservation command centre. It is the long-term destination on the timeline: the conservation project begins by 2040, with a technology and nature ecosystem operational by 2050 and beyond.
